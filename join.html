02 // EMAIL GUIDELINES

Before you email

Briefly introduce yourself, explain your interest in the S2E Lab, use the subject format for your student type, and attach the materials listed below.

Ph.D.

Attach

CV · Transcripts · One-page statement explaining your research interests and how they align with the work done by the lab· Relevant test scores (e.g. TOEFL, GRE, etc), if applicable

Subject [Prospective PhD Student] Semester 20XX

Master’s

Attach

CV · Transcripts No research statement

Subject [Prospective MS Student] Semester 20XX

Undergraduate

Attach

CV · Transcripts No research statement

Subject [Prospective Undergraduate Student] Semester 20XX

For example, if you are a prospective PhD student interested in joining Fall 2027, your e-mail subject would be [Prospective PhD Student] Fall 2027.

Please note: Emailing does not replace the required Ph.D. or STRAND application. Because of the volume of inquiries, Prof. Santos may not be able to reply to every message; please do not send repeated reminders.

03 // HOW WE WORK

A lab built on trust, growth, and good communication.

Hands-on advising

Early in the Ph.D., expect close collaboration as you build research skills and confidence. With experience comes greater independence and ownership.

Weekly momentum

The group meets for weekly stand-ups to share progress, next steps, and blockers. Deeper conversations get the focused time they deserve.

Room to do your best work

You can expect candid, timely feedback; respect for your time; accessible communication; and committed support for your long-term goals.
