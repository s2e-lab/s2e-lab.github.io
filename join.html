02 // EMAIL MATERIALS

Attach the right documents.

The materials and subject line depend on your student path. Emailing does not replace the required Ph.D. or STRAND application.

Before you send

  • Use the subject-line format shown for your student type.
  • Attach every document requested for your path.
  • Briefly introduce yourself and explain why you are interested in the S2E Lab.
  • Because of the volume of inquiries, Prof. Santos may not be able to reply to every message. Please do not send repeated reminder emails.

What to attach

Ph.D. students
  • Curriculum vitae
  • Transcripts
  • One-page research statement
  • Relevant test scores, if applicable

03 // HOW WE WORK

A lab built on trust, growth, and good communication.

Hands-on advising

Early in the Ph.D., expect close collaboration as you build research skills and confidence. With experience comes greater independence and ownership.

Weekly momentum

The group meets for weekly stand-ups to share progress, next steps, and blockers. Deeper conversations get the focused time they deserve.

Room to do your best work

You can expect candid, timely feedback; respect for your time; accessible communication; and committed support for your long-term goals.
